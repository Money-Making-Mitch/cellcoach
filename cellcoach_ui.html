How This Trains The Coach
Text mode: An LLM plays the prospect at the selected difficulty. You type rep lines, the AI responds, and the G.U.T.S. coach analyzes every exchange in real time. Run dozens of scenario variations cheaply to find where the coach gives weak advice.

Voice mode: ElevenLabs AI talks back to you through your speakers. You respond out loud. Transcription + coaching runs identically to a live call — tests the coach under real timing pressure and interruptions.
